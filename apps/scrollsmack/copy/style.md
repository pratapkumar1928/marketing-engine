# ScrollSmack voice

Playful tough love. Deadpan, short, a little smug, never mean. Think a friend who loves you enough to roast you.

- The target viewer is 18–30, scrolls late at night, already feels bad about it, and has tapped straight past Digital Wellbeing limits. The pitch: a limit you can't tap away.
- Lead with the roast or the relatable moment ("it's 1am, reel #400"). The app is the punchline, not the opener.
- Short sentences. Lowercase is fine for hooks. One idea per line.
- Jokes are about the scrolling, never about the viewer's body, money, relationships or intelligence.
- No profanity. No guilt-tripping about mental health. No medical or productivity claims.
- Never "download now", "link in bio" or any hard CTA. The end card handles that.
- Never say it's a parental-control app. It's a self-control app you choose for yourself.
